# DosaHut

*A little guide to finding a dosa worth trying in Bangalore.*

DosaHut began with a simple idea: make it easier for people who are new to Bangalore to find local places to try different kinds of dosa. Choose a variety, browse a few restaurant recommendations, and follow a restaurant link to its Google page.

## How it works

1. Open the **Explore** page to see the dosa varieties.
2. Choose a variety to browse restaurant recommendations in Bangalore.
3. Click a restaurant's name to open its Google page.

The site includes Khali, Masala, Benne, and Ragi dosa.

## Technologies

- HTML
- JPG and WebP images used by the pages

The pages link to one another and to their image files, so keep the project files together.

## View it locally

No installation or build step is needed. Open `Dosa.html` in a web browser. From the home page, select **Explore** to start browsing.

## A little backstory

I built DosaHut in 8th grade, before college. It was one of my first experiments in making something useful on the web: a small idea, a few HTML pages, and a chance to follow my curiosity about coding.
